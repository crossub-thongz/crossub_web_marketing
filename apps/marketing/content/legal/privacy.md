Crossub provides property management software, including the Cros AI assistant, and optional property management support services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information through our website, portals, applications and services (together, the **Platform**). It applies to property professionals, property owners, tenants, applicants, contractors, inspectors, visitors and other people whose information is handled through Crossub.

We aim to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles and, where applicable, the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Where an agency uses Crossub to manage information about other people, that agency remains responsible for its own privacy obligations and instructions to Crossub, subject to any applicable agreement and law.

## 1. Information we collect

Depending on how the Platform is used, information may include:

- Identity and contact information, such as names, addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers, employer or agency details and account identifiers.
- Property and tenancy records, including property addresses, ownership and occupancy details, rental applications, lease information, inspections, photographs, maintenance requests, communication records and supporting documents.
- Payment and accounting information, including rent, invoices, folio records, transaction references and bank account details when supplied for permitted property management or accounting purposes. Payments may be processed by specialist payment providers; we do not claim to store complete payment card details.
- Information included in emails, uploads, forms, messages, support requests and interactions with Cros AI.
- Technical and usage information such as IP address, device and browser details, login activity, audit logs, cookies and feature usage.
- Other information lawfully provided by a customer or their authorised users for property management purposes, which may occasionally include sensitive personal information. Users should only upload information necessary and lawful for the relevant purpose.

We may receive information directly from individuals, from an agency or property manager using the Platform, from authorised third parties, and through automated technical processes.

## 2. Why we use information

We use personal information where reasonably necessary to:

1. Provide accounts, access controls, software functions, records, reporting and customer support.
2. Support leasing, property inspections, maintenance, rent review, communication and accounting workflows as selected by the customer.
3. Operate Cros AI to understand requests, prepare summaries and draft responses, classify tasks and suggest or perform authorised actions, subject to relevant controls.
4. Arrange and deliver optional Inspection Only, Full Service or other specifically requested services.
5. Process authorised payments and maintain relevant accounting and audit records.
6. Maintain security, investigate suspected misuse, troubleshoot defects and improve platform reliability.
7. Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
8. Communicate service notices and, where permitted by law and applicable preferences, information about optional Crossub services.

## 3. Your selected service determines human access

**Self-Manage software:** Customers manage their own property operations. Crossub's Malaysia-based administration team does **not** routinely access or perform property management work on Self-Manage customer records. Limited authorised access by Crossub personnel or service providers may nevertheless be necessary for technical support requested by the customer, security, incident response or legal compliance, subject to access controls and applicable law.

**Inspection Only:** When a customer orders an inspection service, authorised Crossub personnel, including our Malaysia-based administration team where required, may access information reasonably necessary to schedule, coordinate, perform, review and communicate about that inspection.

**Full Service:** When a customer engages Full Service, authorised Crossub personnel, including our Malaysia-based administration team where required, may access information reasonably necessary to perform the agreed property management support activities.

Selecting paid services does not provide unlimited access to unrelated customer information. We aim to apply role-based permissions and access limited to operational needs.

## 4. Australian hosting and overseas processing

Our primary application hosting and database infrastructure use **Amazon Web Services (AWS) in an Australian region**. This describes our primary hosting location and is **not a promise that every data transfer, backup, subprocess or access event occurs exclusively within Australia**.

Crossub uses third-party technology providers, including AI services from **OpenAI (ChatGPT-related technology)** and **Anthropic (Claude-related technology)**. Information necessary for an AI request may be sent to or processed through those providers and their authorised infrastructure, which may be outside Australia or New Zealand. Other vendors used for email delivery, support, analytics or payments may also involve overseas processing, depending on their configuration.

For **Inspection Only and Full Service**, authorised personnel located in **Malaysia** may remotely access relevant records to deliver the purchased service. This overseas operational access is **not part of routine Self-Manage delivery**.

Before applicable overseas disclosures, we take reasonable steps required by law to assess relevant providers and put appropriate contractual, technical and organisational safeguards in place. AI-generated information may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable for a particular legal or operational situation. Customers should apply appropriate professional review, especially for tenancy law, safety, financial, trust accounting, notices and decisions affecting individuals.

We do not state that third-party providers train their models using Crossub data. Their treatment of submitted information depends on our actual product, API agreements, settings and applicable terms. **[Confirm Crossub uses commercial/API arrangements and the relevant data-training and retention configuration.]**

## 6. When we share information

We may share relevant information with:

- The agency, its authorised users and people it permits to participate in the property management workflow.
- Owners, tenants, applicants, inspectors, contractors and other parties as reasonably required for authorised tasks.
- Our contracted hosting, AI, communications, payment and security service providers, subject to appropriate arrangements.
- Crossub service personnel in Australia or, for selected paid services, Malaysia, within their authorised duties.
- Regulators, courts, law enforcement and other recipients where required or permitted by law.

We do not sell personal information as a data-broker business. Disclosure to an optional service provider does not itself mean that person receives access to all records in the customer account.
